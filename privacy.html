Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Who we are

moderngb.net is run by Modern GB, which makes apps and reference sites for stamp collectors. For anything about this policy, email beta@stampdecoder.app.

What we collect

This site has no accounts, forms or shop, so we don't ask you for any personal information.

Analytics, only if you agree. When you first visit, we ask whether you're happy for us to use Google Analytics. If you accept, Google Analytics counts visits and shows us which pages are useful and which links people follow. It sets cookies whose names start with _ga. We have turned off Google signals and advertising features, so the data is not used for advertising. We keep it for 14 months. If you reject, no analytics script is loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

We remember your choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask on every page. .

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser requests a page, and may keep it in its server logs for security.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to delete it. As we don't hold anything that identifies you, there is usually nothing to send, but email us and we'll check. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
